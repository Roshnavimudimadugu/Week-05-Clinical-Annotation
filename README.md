# Week-05-Clinical-Annotation

## Overview

This repository contains my Week 5 bioinformatics training work focused on clinical genomics, clinical variant annotation and evidence-based variant interpretation.

The work combines knowledge from NHS genomic medicine resources with practical annotation and interpretation of selected genetic variants using genomic databases and variant-classification guidance.

## Learning Objectives

The main objectives of Week 5 were to:

- Understand the NHS Genomic Medicine Service and clinical genomic testing context
- Understand the National Genomic Test Directory
- Understand clinical variant annotation
- Interpret molecular consequences of genetic variants
- Use clinical and population genomic databases
- Understand ClinVar clinical classifications and review status
- Use gnomAD for population frequency information
- Understand ClinGen gene-disease validity
- Understand ACGS guidance for variant classification
- Understand why variant interpretation is evidence-based

## Example Variant

One of the variants examined in greater detail was:

`NM_024596.5:c.2253C>G (p.Tyr751*)`

in the **MCPH1** gene.

The variant was annotated as a stop-gained consequence and was considered alongside gene-disease validity, loss-of-function mechanism, population information and variant-classification guidance.

## Evidence-Based Interpretation

A major learning outcome from this week was understanding that molecular consequence and clinical significance are different concepts.

Variant interpretation requires consideration of multiple types of evidence, including:

- Molecular consequence
- Population frequency
- Clinical database information
- Gene-disease validity
- Disease mechanism
- Relevant transcript
- Functional or clinical evidence where available
- Established variant-classification criteria

By completing this work, I developed an understanding of:

2. The process of annotating genetic variants.
3. The difference between molecular consequence and clinical significance.
4. The role of ClinVar and gnomAD in variant interpretation.
5. The importance of gene-disease validity.
6. The use of ACGS and ClinGen guidance.
