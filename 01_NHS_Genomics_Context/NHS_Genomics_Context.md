# NHS Genomics Context

## 1. NHS Genomics 101 Courses Completed

### Course 1 — Genomics in Healthcare
**Genomics 101: Genomics in Healthcare — NHS Genomics Education Programme**

This course introduced the role of genomics in healthcare and how genomic information can support diagnosis, treatment and patient care.

### Course 2 — Investigating the Genome Part 2: The Tests
**Genomics 101: Investigating the Genome Part 2 — The Tests**

This course introduced genomic testing approaches, including sequencing-based tests and the process of generating genomic results.

Certificates for both courses are included in this folder.

---

## 2. NHS Genomic Medicine Service

The NHS Genomic Medicine Service provides a national framework for delivering genomic testing within the NHS.

It supports the use of genomic information in healthcare and aims to ensure that appropriate patients can access appropriate genomic tests through an evidence-led approach.

---

## 3. National Genomic Test Directory

The National Genomic Test Directory provides a framework for genomic tests commissioned by the NHS.

The directory links genomic tests to specific clinical indications and helps define which tests are appropriate for particular clinical situations.

The directory should be understood as a clinical testing framework rather than a list that needs to be memorised.

---

## 4. Clinical Indications

A clinical indication describes the clinical reason or situation for requesting a genomic test.

The indication helps determine whether a particular genomic test is appropriate for a patient.

---

## 5. Patient Eligibility

Genomic testing is not automatically appropriate for every patient.

Eligibility depends on the relevant clinical indication and the criteria associated with the genomic test.

---

## 6. Genomic Laboratory Hubs

Genomic Laboratory Hubs are responsible for delivering genomic laboratory testing within the NHS genomic medicine system.

They support the processing and interpretation of genomic tests and contribute to the delivery of genomic testing services.

---

## 7. Evidence-Led Test Selection

Genomic testing should be selected based on the clinical indication, patient eligibility and available evidence.

The aim is to use an appropriate test for the clinical question rather than selecting tests without a defined clinical purpose.

---

## 8. Key Learning

The main concepts learned from the NHS Genomics modules and supporting NHS resources were:

- Genomics can contribute to diagnosis and patient care.
- Different genomic testing approaches are available for different clinical questions.
- The NHS Genomic Medicine Service provides a framework for genomic testing in NHS healthcare.
- The National Genomic Test Directory links genomic tests with clinical indications.
- Patient eligibility is important when selecting a genomic test.
- Genomic Laboratory Hubs play an important role in delivering genomic laboratory testing.
- Genomic test selection should be evidence-led and clinically appropriate.
