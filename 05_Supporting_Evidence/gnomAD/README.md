# gnomAD Evidence

## Purpose

This folder contains supporting evidence obtained from gnomAD during the Week 5 clinical variant annotation process.

## Contents

The files document gnomAD searches for selected variants and relevant regional population-data coverage.

## Information Considered

gnomAD was used to investigate:

- Whether an exact variant was present
- Population allele-frequency information where available
- Regional sequencing coverage

## MCPH1 Example

For the MCPH1 variant:

`NM_024596.5:c.2253C>G (p.Tyr751*)`

the exact variant was not found in the searched gnomAD dataset.

The result was recorded as:

"Variant not found; allele frequency not available."

This was not interpreted as an allele frequency of zero.

## Role in Variant Interpretation

Population frequency information can provide evidence that helps assess whether a variant is compatible with the expected frequency of a disease-associated allele.

However, absence of a variant from gnomAD alone does not establish pathogenicity.

> These materials are retained for bioinformatics training and documentation purposes and do not represent patient-specific clinical interpretation.
