# ClinGen MCPH1 Evidence

## Purpose

This folder contains supporting resources used to investigate the MCPH1 gene-disease relationship and loss-of-function evidence relevant to the MCPH1 variant analysed during Week 5.

## MCPH1 Variant

The main variant considered was:

`NM_024596.5:c.2253C>G (p.Tyr751*)`

This variant produces a stop-gained consequence.

## Contents

The supporting resources in this folder include material related to:

- MCPH1 gene-disease validity
- ClinGen curation
- MCPH1 disease association
- Autosomal recessive inheritance
- Loss-of-function mechanism
- ClinGen SVI PVS1 guidance
- MCPH1 transcript information
- MCPH1 exon information

## Role of the Evidence

These resources were used to understand the relationship between:

- Variant molecular consequence
- MCPH1 gene-disease validity
- Loss-of-function mechanism
- Transcript relevance
- Nonsense-mediated decay considerations
- PVS1 evidence

## Interpretation

The resources support the evidence-based analysis of the MCPH1 variant and demonstrate how gene-disease validity and loss-of-function evidence can be considered during variant interpretation.

The evidence was considered alongside other resources such as SnpEff, ClinVar, gnomAD and ACGS guidance.

> These materials are retained for bioinformatics training and documentation purposes and do not represent a patient-specific clinical interpretation.
